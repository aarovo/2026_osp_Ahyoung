The Best Pastel de Nata I Found in Porto

A tiny custard tart that stopped me, May 28, 2026

Tucked into a quiet side street in Porto, Castro serves a pastel de nata with a shatteringly thin, flaky shell and a warm, barely-set custard center, its top scorched in just the right places. No fuss, no crowds, just a small counter doing one thing perfectly.

Sandboarding Down the Dunes of the Sahara

Chasing golden light and losing my footing entirely, May 7, 2026

We arrived at the dunes as the sun dipped low, riding camels out before climbing up for a sunset sandboarding run, wobblier and funnier than expected. That night we ate slow-cooked tagine under a sky thick with stars, the whole day feeling almost unreal. Sand, camels, and stars all in one unforgettable stretch of desert.
